# WAMV-2 Overview

WAMV-2 is a WAM-V 16 autonomous surface vehicle configured as a sensor-rich survey and research platform.

- No bow thrusters; relies on main thrusters for maneuvering.
- Equipped with a dedicated depth sensor and weather station for environmental and bathymetric measurements.
- Includes mounting points for a waterwheel and a drone platform, enabling additional experiments and payloads.
