# WAMV-2 Sensors and Payloads

## Navigation Sensors

- GNSS/INS: make, model, mounting location.
- IMUs, compasses, or other inertial sensors.

## Environmental Sensors

- Depth sensor: model, range, mounting, and important notes.
- Weather station: model, measured variables, mounting.

## Payloads

- Waterwheel: purpose and physical integration.
- Drone platform: mounting and any interfaces.

## Calibration Notes

- Any extra calibration required for depth and weather sensors.
